Plans: Free, Essential, Premium and Premium Agenda

Session Flow plans: Free, Essential, Premium and Premium Agenda, with online booking, monthly or yearly, and the 14-day free trial through the store.

10.1 — What's in the free plan?

Up to 4 active records

Full Today, Schedule and Notifications screens

One-off sessions and time-slot reservation

Full backup (import + export)

WhatsApp reminders (with a small "Generated by Session Flow" signature)

Days off: up to 14 days/year, day by day

10.2 — What does Premium add?

Cloud: your data on all your devices (section 14)

Up to 1000 active clients, and unlimited records of one-off sessions (2.7)

Insights: your business in charts on big screens (computer, iPad and tablets)

WhatsApp billing (no signature)

File for the Receita: Carnê-Leão and the Receita Saúde receipts at once, in Brazil (8.9)

WhatsApp reminders (no signature)

Days off by period, unlimited

Record type: Group

Prepaid payments

Per-session discount

Schedule filters (paid, outstanding, events and more)

Full statistics and the settlement history are in Premium and Essential; each record's History tab is in every plan.

10.3 — How much does Premium cost?

Pricing varies by region. Check the up-to-date values on the Subscription screen inside the app, where the plans available for your country are listed.

10.4 — How does the free trial work?

The trial is run by the store (Google Play or the App Store), not by the app. When you tap "Subscribe monthly" or "Subscribe yearly" on the Subscription screen (on any of the plans), the store opens the subscription with 14 days at no charge. To avoid paying, cancel in the store before the trial ends — on iPhone, at least 24 hours before (see 10.5). If you don't cancel, the store starts charging for the subscription when the 14 days are up.

10.5 — How do I cancel my subscription?

Through the app store:

Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → Session Flow → Cancel.

iOS: Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Session Flow → Cancel.

The app can't cancel directly — the store manages it.

10.6 — I cancelled. Do my data disappear?

No. But you lose access to the functional part of the app. When you open it, you'll see a screen requiring one of these actions: subscribe again, restore subscription, pick 4 records to keep on the free plan, or back up if you want to reinstall and subscribe later. If you used the cloud, its data is kept for 90 days after the plan ends and then deleted; what is on your device is not touched.

10.7 — I already subscribed. How do I restore on another phone?

Subscription screen → "Restore purchase". The app checks your store account and reactivates Premium automatically. With the cloud, Premium also works on any device where you sign in with the same account.

10.8 — I have a redeem code (coupon). Where do I use it?

Depends on the platform:

iOS: in the app, Settings → Subscription → "Redeem code".

Android: when subscribing, choose the subscription that matches the code (yearly or monthly). Instead of paying, tap the payment method and select "I have a code". It's important to choose the correct subscription that the code applies to.

10.9 — What about the Essential plan?

Essential (monthly or yearly) includes up to 40 active records, full statistics and the settlement history, with no brand in reminders and documents. WhatsApp billing, groups, prepaid payments, per-session discount, days off by period, advanced schedule filters, up to 20 custom templates, the cloud and Insights are Premium features. See the full comparison in Settings → Subscription → Compare plans.

10.10 — When does the app show me Premium?

When you tap something your plan doesn't include, the app shows what you would get, with your own data: at the 5th record, the plans that fit (closing the record of someone you no longer see also frees a spot); in "Charge", the message Premium would send on WhatsApp; in Statistics, one line with what Essential and Premium show there. On its own, the app offers the trial only once, in a card on Today when your first steps are done, with the day of the first charge if you subscribe and the last day to cancel in the store; and, in Outstanding, a strip that hides for 30 days with the "×". Never on the day you install the app, and at most one new invitation a week.

10.11 — What about Premium Agenda?

Everything in Premium plus your online booking page, at agenda.getsessionflow.app/your-name. On it, clients pick their time, 24/7, and only your free times show up (your appointments, days off and every event scheduled in the app make that time unavailable). Booked times go into your schedule, the person who booked gets the email confirmation and reminder, and you get an email for every booking (or a daily summary). Under "See more features", the rest, such as the cancellation deadline and the printable QR code card. Outside healthcare, the page can also ask for a deposit, and it only shows your Pix key: whoever books sends the Pix on their own, and you check your bank and mark the deposit under "Already paid", on the request sheet. The app doesn't receive or detect payments. Premium Agenda is on the Subscription screen, after Premium yearly, monthly or yearly, at your country's store price, and "See what the page looks like" shows an example page and how it works. If the store doesn't have the plan yet, the price line says "Not available in this store right now". Like Essential and Premium, it starts with 14 free days, monthly or yearly (see 10.4). If you already have Premium and move to Premium Agenda, it's a plan change: the store ends Premium and uses what's left of it, without charging for both. Going the other way (from Premium Agenda to Premium or Essential), the screen first shows what you'd lose. In Settings → Online booking, if you don't have the plan yet, you see what the page does, with "See plans". When you subscribe to Premium Agenda, the app opens Settings → Online booking right away, so you can start with your hours.

In Settings → Online booking → How you appear, below the page address, "Show on Google" starts off: only people with the link reach the page. On, the page can show up in Google searches (Google takes a few days to find it and, if you turn it off again, a few days to drop it); each person's links (the booking one, the follow-up one and the block one) never show up. In healthcare, it only turns on with your professional license, which appears on the page: on Google, the page can count as advertising for your licensing board, and in Brazil, doctors who state a specialty add the RQE next to the license. If you turned it on before saying you work in healthcare, it shows as off: turn it on again for the page to go back to searches.

Once your page has an address, the app gets the Bookings tab, with the number of things that need a look: requests waiting for your answer (each one says until when it holds the time), what changed through the link (with "OK"), who booked through the page from now on and, under "History", the last 30 days. On the phone, it sits right after Today, in place of Documents, which moves to Settings → Your data (without the Today screen, right after Clients, and Documents stays in the bar); on the computer, in the bar on the left, right after Today, with the tapped request next to the list.

The page follows the app: what you book, cancel or change reaches it within seconds, also when the app goes to the background (phone locked, another app). The time of someone coming back from a pause becomes unavailable on the page from the return date on. If someone books through the page a time you had just filled in the app, before the page knew, the session goes in anyway (the person already got the confirmation), and the app tells you with a notification and the line "… booked through your page at …’s time", in Today and in Bookings: "View booking" opens the session to reschedule or cancel it (the person who booked is told, as always), and "OK" removes the line from this device. If the person who booked has a repeat and is closed or on a break, the record stays as it is: the line "… booked, but the record is closed" (or "is on a break"), in Today and Bookings, holds the time until you decide, and "Reactivate" brings the repeat back. Without a repeat, the person comes back on their own. Accepting a request at a time that got taken asks first, and the request says when the day is a day off. If your page is left with the old times (something is missing for it to go live, or publishing has failed for more than 30 minutes), Today and Bookings say "Your page still shows your old times", with "Open online booking".

On the sheet of a request that is waiting, "Suggest another time" sends the person up to 3 free times: your page times, from the requested day on (the first one after the request comes selected), or any other, through "Another day and time". The requested time goes back on your page right away, and the suggested ones are held for the person for up to 24 hours (or until each one starts): nobody else books them through the page, and the app warns you if you reschedule or book another session over them ("This time is on hold"). The same warning covers a new repeat, a day off, an event and the calendar import. The person gets an email and keeps one through the link, without filling anything in again: once chosen, it goes into your schedule as an accepted request; with a deposit, the request goes back to "To answer", and you accept it after you check your bank and see the Pix. Meanwhile, the request sits under "Waiting for a choice", which doesn’t count in the tab badge, with "Cancel the suggestion" (the person gets "Request declined"). With no choice, "Changes through the page" says the suggestion expired; if the person says none of them work, that they didn’t want the suggested times. If the email with the times bounces, the sent suggestion has "Send the link on WhatsApp", with the link to choose.

On a full day — a day you work, with no open time for the chosen service —, the page offers "Let me know if a time opens up": the person leaves their name and email (confirmed with the code) and joins that day's waiting list. Never on a day off, on a day you don't work or on today. If a time opens up on that day (a cancellation, a reschedule, a day off removed, a request declined or expired), everyone waiting gets an email at once, and whoever books first gets it: the link in the email opens the page on that day, with the name and email already filled in, and the WhatsApp is asked when booking. Each person gets at most 3 notices; whoever books that day leaves the list, and everything goes away at the end of the day. In Bookings, "Waiting list" shows each day with how many people are waiting (not counted in the tab badge); a tap shows each person's name, email and when they joined. The list comes on: to turn it off, Settings → Online booking → Bookings → "The waiting list" (whoever was waiting leaves the list).

When you cancel or reschedule in the app a session that came through the page, the person who booked is told: they get the email ("Booking cancelled" or the new time), and the booking link and the reminder change too. A pause, ending the client and finishing a block ("Finish the block", "End midway") that take a session from the page off the schedule also tell them, and the slot goes back to the link; if the pause is removed after the email, that time stays cancelled. The session menu says when. With a deposit, the app first asks who cancelled: "I cancelled" (the email offers whoever paid a full refund or a credit, their choice) or that the person asked (the email says "Booking cancelled, as you asked", and the deposit follows your cancellation deadline, as when the person cancels through the link); then "And the deposit?" follows the answer. Once you reschedule a session from the page that has a deposit, if the person cancels — through the link, at any time, or by asking you — the choice is theirs: a full refund of the deposit or a credit; the link, the emails and "And the deposit?" say so. When the email doesn't go out (a request waiting for your answer holds the new time, or too many changes on the same day), "Changes through the page" says so.

If an email to the person who booked bounces (the address rejected it) or is marked as spam, nothing else goes to that address for 12 months, and "Changes through the page" says so ("…’s email bounced" or "… marked the email as spam", with "OK", in the tab’s number). The booking shows "email bounced", and its sheet has "Send the link on WhatsApp": the message ready, with the time and a new link to view, reschedule or cancel. On a waiting request, the sheet says your answer won’t arrive by email; the day’s reminders (the bell in Today) say "email doesn’t arrive"; the session menu says the email won’t go. When the address of the person booking no longer gets Session Flow emails, the request sheet says "Email doesn’t reach this address": let them know on WhatsApp.

A follow-up booked through the follow-up link (see the follow-up question, under Records) shows up in "Upcoming through the page" as "Follow-up · through the follow-up link". Its sheet says the link doesn’t ask for a name or email: no email reminder, with the WhatsApp from your records; rescheduled in the app, the link shows the new time; cancelled, the follow-up link works again. A session booked through the block link (see the block question, under Records) shows up as "14:00 · 6/10 · through the block link", and its sheet says the same; cancelled in the app, the spot goes back to the link.

A record has an email (optional), below WhatsApp: in the record › Profile and in the form; the search in Clients also finds by email. Whoever books through the page is recognized by WhatsApp or, if the WhatsApp is different, by the email confirmed with the code that matches the record, with the same first name. By email, a closed record doesn't count. When someone who is already a client books with an email the record doesn’t have, the request sheet (and the sheet of a booking made right away) shows "New email", with "Save to the record" or "Replace with the new one"; recognized by email, "New WhatsApp". Nothing changes on its own: the page doesn’t check the WhatsApp, and you see the name and the contact before tapping. The activity log notes the change, "through online booking", and "Undo" brings the previous one back. Someone booking for the first time comes in with the confirmed email. If an email to the person who booked bounces, the record shows "bounced on" and the date below the email.

Notices go to your account email. If one bounces, or if you mark it as spam, the next ones stop, and Settings → Online booking → Notices shows "Notices aren’t reaching your email", with the reason. Check the address, or mark the notice as not spam, before tapping "Resume notices": the app asks the server to take only your account email off the list, at most once a month.
