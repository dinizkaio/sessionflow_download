Statistics and reports

Session Flow statistics: received and forecast, what's still open, the average ticket, the spreadsheet for your accountant and Insights, with its reminders.

8.1 — Where do I see how much I received this month?

Statistics tab → "Overview", by month, week or day (the adjust button at the top shows these options). Shows:

Received: the money that came in during the period.

Forecast (month): what falls due in the month. For clients who pay after their sessions, it is the previous month's sessions. With "Your schedule" set to "Occasional clients", in a month that has not started yet, it is called "Already booked": what is already on your calendar, and its sheet says "What’s already booked". In a month when an instalment of a priced block falls due, it stays "Forecast": the instalment is not a booked time. With "Both", the Received and Forecast sheets show each side below the title (recurring and one-off sessions; in a month that has not started yet, the one-off sessions already booked), the same sides as in Insights.

Value of sessions (week and day): what the period's sessions are worth, paid or not, before or after. Tap the amount to see, per client, the sessions. With a fixed monthly fee, it also shows the average per session: the fee split across the month's sessions.

Outstanding: what hasn't been paid yet.

8.2 — Can I see day-by-day evolution?

Yes, in the Overview chart. The full feature (with average ticket, cancellation rate and the evolution chart) is in the Essential and Premium plans. On the free plan you see the essential numbers.

8.3 — What's the "settlement history"?

It's at the bottom of the "Outstanding" tab. In each year, the months go from the most recent to the oldest, each with how many sessions were paid ("88 of 90") and how much is still outstanding, in amber. The current month says "in progress": sessions still to come don't count as late. The three most recent months and those with something outstanding are in view; the others, already settled, under "See the other months". A fully paid year is a single line. The history always adds up to the "To receive" total: a month that hasn't started yet but already has something outstanding (the custom rule "N days before the start of the month") is at the top, with since when; the years before the last five are in one line at the bottom ("2021 and earlier"), which opens them. Tap a month to open its sheet: who has something outstanding, with "Mark as paid" (it opens the sheet with what's outstanding from that month, so you can check it and use any credit), and the paid sessions, with who paid and when. In the Essential and Premium plans.

8.4 — How do I see received payments?

In Statistics, tap the amount received for the period (month, week or day). "Received payments" opens, with each payment grouped by the day it came in.

8.5 — Can I edit a payment date?

Yes. Open the client's profile (tap the name on any screen) → "History" tab → under "Payments", tap the month → on the payment, tap "⋯" → "Change the date". Useful when you logged the payment late but want to keep the right date. For a monthly month, the date is the whole month's; the extras' difference, received later, keeps its own date. Each payment's "Reverse" is in the same "⋯".

8.6 — Does the "Outstanding" tab list all clients with pending payments?

Yes. It's your dashboard of what's outstanding: it lists all records with unpaid sessions up to the selected month, adding what's still outstanding from earlier months, not just the current one. The month goes up to the current one: what falls due later isn't outstanding yet. Each card shows the billing type (and the change, when there is one), the pause, the closing and the credit, with "Mark as paid" at hand; closed clients with something outstanding are at the bottom, under "Closed clients".

8.7 — Is there an average ticket and cancellation rate?

Yes. Average ticket = the value of the period's sessions, paid or not, divided by the number of sessions (free sessions count as zero; written-off ones are left out). Cancellation rate = % of the period's sessions that were cancelled, out of all of the period's sessions, including those still to come (vacations and pauses don't count as cancellations). % paid = how many of the period's sessions are already paid; in a period that has not started yet, the number is in the text color, without the amber (nothing could have been paid yet), and the green, from 80% up, stays. With "Your schedule" set to "Occasional clients", new people take the place of "% paid": who came for the first time in the period, counted as in "Who came" in Insights (8.11). Tap to see who came: first-timers at the top, and each person with how many times they came; tapping a name opens their profile. In a period that has not started yet, new people don't show. In the Essential and Premium plans.

8.8 — How do I send the month's payments to my accountant?

In Statistics, tap the amount received for the month and, below the list, "Spreadsheet for your accountant". You get a spreadsheet (it opens in Excel and Google Sheets) with each payment: the date it came in, the name (the full one, from the Documents details), the tax ID, the legal guardian, the payer's tax ID, the group, what it was for and the amount. It is the money that came in, as in "Received": a session paid with credit doesn't come in again (the credit came in on the day of the deposit). In the week or day view, the spreadsheet covers that period. The file only leaves the device through sharing, to wherever you choose. For the Receita (Carnê-Leão and the Receita Saúde receipts), see 8.9.

8.9 — How do I issue the Receita Saúde receipts all at once? Premium

With the Country set to Brazil (Settings → Date, time and currency → Country; on automatic, with the currency in reais or the phone in Brazil): in Statistics, tap the amount received for the month and, below the list, "File for the Receita". You get the file that Carnê-Leão Web imports: in e-CAC, Carnê-Leão → Escrituração → Importar Escrituração. "Analisar Arquivo" checks it without recording anything; "Importar" issues the Receita Saúde receipts for all the payments in the file at once (for occupations that don't issue receipts, it only records the payments).

In the sheet, choose your occupation, the same as in the Identificação tab of your Carnê-Leão. The receipt carries your CPF (Documents → Settings → Professional details), the CPF of the person seen and, when someone else pays, the payer's (the Documents details of the client; see 13.4). A payment without the CPF stays out until you fill it in: tap the row. With more than one active council registration, enter the one for the receipt in "Registration on the receipt".

A payment imported twice is recorded twice. After sharing, the app asks "Imported it into Carnê-Leão?": once imported, mark the payments as sent, and they come unchecked in the next file (also on your other devices with the cloud). "Select all", next to the payments title, checks the ones that can go and haven't been sent yet; one already sent is only checked one by one. A receipt you already issued in the Receita app would also be doubled: uncheck it in the sheet. Made a mistake? "Desfazer escrituração", in the Carnê-Leão Histórico da Escrituração, within 10 days. One file per year, with up to 1000 payments. The file only leaves the device through sharing.

8.10 — What does Insights remind me of? Premium

In Insights (computer, iPad and tablets), the "Reminders" card starts with "What needs attention": one line per person, with their most urgent reminder and "+1" when there's another (the profile shows the whole person). Tapping the line opens the profile.

What it reminds you of:

  • the end of a pause, in the next 7 days;
  • a repeat ending within 14 days, with no other to continue it;
  • credit that covers only one more session, or that ran out;
  • what's been outstanding for over 30 days: the 3 people outstanding the longest, with the amount and the "since" from the Outstanding tab;
  • a no-show at the last 2 sessions;
  • 3 sessions cancelled in 8 weeks (not counting time off or pauses);
  • no sessions scheduled in the next 35 days, for those with a repeat;
  • someone in initial sessions with nothing scheduled for 30 days;
  • a pause with no return date for 60 days;
  • a block near its end (2 sessions or fewer to go) and a stalled block (sessions left to schedule and nothing scheduled for 14 days);
  • birthdays, in the next 14 days (the date of birth from Documents).

With the currency set to the Brazilian real, at the top, a national holiday in the next 30 days with sessions booked and no day off, on a line for the day: the name and the date ("All Souls' Day · Mon, Nov 2") and, below, "Holiday with 3 sessions booked and no day off". "Mark day off" (or tapping the line) opens the day off for that day, with the holiday's name; the sheet shows who has a session before you confirm.

Below, the years with you and who has gone over a year without a price change. The limits are written at the foot of the card ("How we count").

The "×" at the end of the line ("Hide this reminder") takes the reminder it shows off the list (with the cloud, on all your devices) until what it says changes (the person's next one, if any, takes its place): the pause gets another date, a new no-show, a new credit deposit, the outstanding amount is paid. A long pause, stalled initial sessions, "no sessions scheduled", a stalled block and the price change don't change on their own: they come back in 90 days. Right after the "×", the bar has "Undo", which also works on all of them.

8.11 — I see occasional clients: what does Insights show? Premium

With "Your schedule" set to "Occasional clients", Insights has the order and the cards for occasional clients. At the top: the sessions held, the people who came (and how many for the first time), the value per session and no-shows.

  • "What a session brings in": the average value of the sessions of each month, against a year earlier, the value per hour worked and the service that brings in the most per hour. An included follow-up counts as a session worth zero, with its time (also in the service that includes it).
  • "Who came": the people in the period and how many for the first time, and how many of the first-timers came back within 60 days. Coming means having a session held, not a no-show; an included follow-up is not a visit (it is part of one); in the period, each person counts once. On the phone, the new people in the Overview (8.7) are the first-timers here.
  • "The next visit": how many visits ended with the next one already booked (another session for the same person, created in the app on the day of the visit). Below, who is due back, as in Clients: nothing booked and the last visit more than one and a half times the usual gap ago. With this card, "Due for a session" leaves the reminders.
  • "Top services": the services (2.18) of the sessions held, from the most booked to the least, with what each one brought in.
  • "How far ahead they book": in 100 squares, the sessions booked on the same day, 1 or 2 days ahead, 3 to 7 and more than 7, counted from when the session was created in the app (a page request, from when you accepted it).
  • "The next 14 days", in place of "Coming months": the sessions on your calendar, day by day, with a tick for what was usually booked this far ahead over the last 13 weeks (after your first 4 weeks in the app).

The data decides what shows: "Outstanding today", when there is some; "Payment timing", when fewer than 90 in 100 arrive by the day; the new cards, with at least 30 sessions in the period. In the reminders, "Years with you" and "No price change in over a year" count only people with recurring sessions, and so does the Clients card (who arrived and who left): with no one like that, it does not appear. With "Your schedule" set to "Both", a filter at the top picks All, Recurring or Occasional. "Recurring" is Insights for people with recurring sessions, with only them; "Occasional", Insights for occasional clients, with only those people. In "All", the "Recurring and occasional" card splits income between the two sides, month by month (the two parts add up to the income); "Coming months" counts only recurring sessions (one-off sessions are booked only a few days ahead and show in the next 14 days); and the next visit and how far ahead they book count only one-off sessions. The button in the corner of each card shows the numbers and "How we count".
