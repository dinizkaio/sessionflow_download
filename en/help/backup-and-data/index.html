Backup and data

Your data in Session Flow: backups (with a password, if you like), restoring, the copy kept before each update, a new phone and the activity log.

9.1 — Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on the cloud (Premium plans), a copy is also kept on Google Cloud servers in São Paulo, Brazil, to keep your devices in sync. Without the cloud, nothing leaves your device. Details in section 14.

9.2 — How do I back up?

Settings → Backup & Restore → "Share" or "Save to device" (on the iPhone, "Export backup"; in the browser, "Download backup"). Generates a .json file with everything: records, sessions, settings. Save it somewhere safe (Google Drive, email, USB drive). With "Protect with a password" on, at the top of the same screen, the app asks for a password for each backup and the file is encrypted: without the password, it won't open. The password isn't stored anywhere, and if you forget it, the backup is lost. Without the cloud, the app reminds you to export when the last backup is more than 30 days old.

9.3 — How do I restore a backup?

Settings → Backup & Restore → "Import backup". Pick the .json file. A backup with a password asks for it before opening. Note: importing replaces current data — whatever's in the app gets erased. For 2 days after the import, Backup & Restore shows "Undo the import": the data goes back to how it was before. After that, the copy is deleted. In the browser, before replacing, the app downloads a copy of what is there: to go back, import that file.

9.4 — I switched phones. How do I migrate?

With the cloud on: install the app on the new phone and sign in with the same account in Settings → Cloud & privacy — your data comes down automatically. Without the cloud: back up on the old phone (Settings → Backup & Restore → "Share") → send the file to yourself (email, drive) → install the app on the new phone → Import backup. Everything migrates: records, history, settings.

9.5 — Can I recover data after uninstalling?

With the cloud on, yes: reinstall and sign in with the same account. Without the cloud, only if you backed up first — without a backup, data is lost when you uninstall. That's why we recommend exporting regularly. The phone's automatic backup (Google on Android, iCloud on iPhone) helps in some cases but doesn't replace a manual backup. If the app ever can't open your data, it shows a screen to try again or send the files to support: don't uninstall.

9.6 — Restoring a backup with more than 4 records on the free plan?

The app offers two options:

Subscribe to Premium and import everything (all records come in).

Pick up to 4 records to import (the rest are left out).

9.7 — "Delete all data" — what happens?

Permanently removes from the app: all records, sessions, payments, schedules, settings, the document details (your profile, the templates and each record's details), the activity log and the saved copies (from before the cloud, of what was in the cloud, from before the last import and from before the last update). It cannot be undone. With the cloud on for this device, the cloud data is deleted too — the app asks you to sign in again to confirm; your other devices stop syncing and keep what they already have. If you have a saved backup, you can import it to recover the data.

9.8 — What is the Activity log?

A list of what was done in the app, newest first: each payment received or reverted, session rescheduled, no-show recorded or undone, session cancelled or restored, extra session, pause, closing, reactivation, price and billing changes, and each "Charge" that opened WhatsApp with a payment request (5.10; the app doesn't know whether the message was sent). Each day is a block; inside it, each record's actions, with the time, what it was and the amount on the right (+ what came in, − what went out). Tapping an action shows the details: the full sentence, when it happened, on which kind of device (Android, iPhone, iPad or browser), who did it and the "Open profile" button. In a wide window (a computer, an iPad), the details sit beside the list, with the action highlighted, and change with each tap. Nothing is deleted or edited line by line: undoing a payment adds a new line.

━━ Where to see it ━━

  • Settings → Activity log: everything, with the type filters in one line, the period below, next to the count, and the magnifying glass at the top to search by name. The share button at the top exports what the filter shows as a spreadsheet.
  • On the Today screen, the clock with the arrow at the top (where the bell used to be; with Today hidden, on Clients).
  • In each record, the clock with the arrow at the top, next to the bell, opens the log of that record only.
  • In the Sessions tab, tapping a session and choosing "What happened to this session" shows only that session's lines — including its reschedules.

━━ Good to know ━━

  • The amount on the right is the money that came in or went out. With monthly billing, the payment is for the month: a single line with the month's amount, not one per session. What was paid with credit already came in on the day of the deposit: the line shows only the rest, and the details say how much came from credit.
  • What was done before the version that brought the log is assembled from what the app already kept (payments, reschedules, no-shows, cancellations, pauses, prices and billing), with a clock in place of the time (in the details, "Before the log") and without a device.
  • What the app does on its own is there too (in the details, "The app, on its own"): the sessions a day off cancels and the repairs it makes when it opens or when something arrives from the cloud.
  • It does not enter any calculation (outstanding amounts, statistics, documents): it is only for reading.
  • With the cloud on, it syncs with the other data; it goes in the backup; deleting a record deletes its log; "Delete all data" deletes everything.
  • It is in every plan.

9.9 — No visits in over 2 years: what to do with the data?

Data protection laws ask that data be kept only while it’s useful. In Settings → Your data, the line "No visits in over 2 years" shows up when someone has had no session in over 2 years, has nothing booked and nothing outstanding (anyone with an outstanding amount stays out). Once a year, on opening, the app tells you how many there are. The app only suggests: nothing goes unless you choose.

━━ The two options ━━

  • "Remove personal data": the name, the WhatsApp, the room link, the custom messages, the notes (the sessions’ too) and the Documents details (tax ID, address, date of birth) are removed. The name becomes the record word and the year of the last session ("Client from 2023"); in a group, each participant becomes "Participant 1", "Participant 2". The sessions and payments stay: the Statistics don’t change. The record is closed, if it wasn’t already.
  • "Delete": everything goes, like "Delete record" in the profile. First, the confirmation says what changes in the Statistics (the years and how much Received goes down).

Neither can be undone, and both reach your other devices through the cloud. Backups you saved before still have the data: the app can’t reach those files. So do the copies the app saves on its own on this device (9.7), until they’re deleted: the one from before the last update stays up to 7 days (9.10); the others, up to 2.

9.10 — I updated the app: can I go back to the data from before?

Yes, for 7 days. The first time a new version opens, before it touches the data, the app saves a copy of the data from before it on this device (with no records, there's nothing to save). It shows up in Settings → Backup & Restore → "Go back to the data from before the update", with the date and the version. Going back replaces this device's data with the copy: whatever changed after that is removed from it. With the cloud on, this device leaves the cloud first (your account stays signed in); the cloud and your other devices don't change, and when you turn the cloud back on, the app asks what to do with the data on each side. The copy stays on this device (it doesn't go to the app's cloud) and is deleted after 7 days, once it's used or with "Delete all data". If another new version arrives within those 7 days, the copy stays the one from before the first. It doesn't replace your backup: export one from time to time (9.2). In the browser, there is no such copy.
