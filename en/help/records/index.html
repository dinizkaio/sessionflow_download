Records (clients, groups and events)

Records in Session Flow: "Schedule a session", standing or one-off, services, follow-ups, priced blocks, groups, events, pausing and closing a record.

2.1 — What's the difference between client, patient, coachee, student?

Just the label shown in the UI. Session Flow supports several record types (patient, client, coachee, student, mentee, analysand, supervisee, and others) to fit your field's vocabulary. They're functionally identical — pick the term that matches your work.

2.2 — Where do I set the default type?

Settings → What you call them → Default record type. That's the type applied automatically to new records, and the word the screens use ("No patients found"). Each individual record can have its own type, different from the default (handy if you serve mixed audiences — therapy + coaching, for example); its own page uses its own word.

2.3 — How do I add a recurring client?

The short way: tap "+" and, in "Schedule a session", choose who and when; under "Repeat", "Every week", "Every two weeks" or "Every month" (2.17). For another repeat (twice a week, for example), monthly billing or a group, use the full record: "Full record", at the end of the booking screen (or "New Client", in the Clients tab, while it is empty). The form comes in four parts, in this order: Client (the name, WhatsApp and email), Billing (the price and "How you bill": the mode and the moment, which come from Settings), Sessions (the repeat: start date, time and frequency) and Reminder. Then tap "Save" (on a phone, in the bar at the bottom, next to the record summary). The app generates all future sessions from the repeats.

As you fill it in, the form shows what will happen: under the price, what the repetition adds up to in a month; under the time, whether it is free in your schedule and the next dates (3.8). If the start date is before today, the app tells you how many sessions already happened and how much will be outstanding, with a button to start on the next date.

On a computer and a tablet in landscape, the form opens in place of the tabs, with "How it will look" next to it: the card as it will appear in Clients, the dates, the price, when it becomes outstanding and the reminder.

2.4 — Can a single client have more than one repeat?

Yes. You can add several repeats (e.g. Tuesdays 2 PM AND Thursdays 4 PM) for the same record: on the form, "Another time" adds one more. Common in therapy plans with variable frequency.

2.5 — How do I create a group? Premium

When choosing the record type, pick "Group". You can register all participants at once. Each participant has independent payment tracking — useful for group therapy, group supervision or class-based sessions.

2.6 — How do I add one-off sessions (no repeat)?

One-off sessions are records with no repeat. When creating the record, under "Sessions", choose "One-off" (instead of "Repeating") and schedule the first one (date and time). You schedule the next ones one at a time: "Schedule a session" on the profile, on the card and in the Today card's menu. When there is no session scheduled and nothing outstanding, the record moves to the "Seen" block in Clients (with "One-off sessions" or "Both" in Your schedule, to "Nothing booked", 2.17), outside the plan limit; scheduling a session brings it back. To start repeating, "Repeat", next to "Schedule a session" on the profile (2.17). For a client with a repeat who needs one more session, use "Add extra session" (3.7).

2.7 — How many records can I have?

Active records count (in a group, each participant). On the free plan: up to 4. On Essential: up to 40. On Premium: up to 1000, and records of one-off sessions don't count: no limit. Paused or closed records don't count, nor records of one-off sessions with nothing booked or outstanding (2.6).

2.8 — How do I pause a client?

Open the client's profile (tap the name on any screen) → Schedule tab → "New pause", and set the period. You can also long-press the card in Clients → "Pause client". During the pause: nothing new becomes outstanding, and sessions scheduled within the period are cancelled automatically. If something already paid falls in the period, the app asks what to do (keep as credit, keep as paid or refund). If you delete the pause (or reactivate before it ends) and a monthly month already paid becomes worth more, the app asks whether to charge the difference: with "Charge the difference", it becomes outstanding; with "Don't charge", the month stays paid as it is. When you resume, counting restarts normally.

Need an appointment in the middle of the pause (an urgent one)? Add an extra session ("Extra session" at the top of the profile, or "Schedule a session"): the app warns you, and the session is charged at the amount you enter, even during the pause; the pause's other sessions stay free of charge. "Reschedule" doesn't move a session to a day of the pause.

On the profile's Sessions tab, the pause shows as a line among the sessions, with the period and how many sessions did not take place (Recent) or will not (Upcoming). On the Schedule tab, pauses in progress or scheduled sit right below the active repeats; finished pauses and ended repeats sit collapsed at the end: tap the title to open them.

2.9 — Can I pause without setting a return date?

Yes — the record stays paused indefinitely. If there is an outstanding amount from before the pause, the app asks what to do with it:

Keep outstanding — the amount stays in To receive until it's paid.

Write off — the amount leaves To receive and everything that adds up money to come in, but it stays on record: the sessions remain in the client file as "Written off" and count in that month's Statistics (they weren't paid). If the client pays later, the payment counts. If they come back, the app lets you know and you choose whether to undo the write-off.

You can also write off (or undo it) later, in the client file › Profile › Status. Written-off amounts are kept in a collapsed block at the end of Outstanding.

2.10 — What's the difference between pausing and closing a client?

It's more about your expectation regarding the record than a technical difference:

Pause — you believe there may be a return. Open-ended pause, no defined end.

Close client — you believe there will be no return.

Both can be reactivated at any time if the person comes back.

Either way, the sessions that already happened stay in Statistics. When you close a client, you choose what to do with what was still outstanding: keep it outstanding (it shows up under Outstanding, in "Closed clients") or write it off (it leaves To receive and stays on record in the client file, as with a pause).

2.11 — How do I reactivate a paused or closed record?

To find the record, go to the records list and scroll all the way to the end — paused or closed records appear in that part of the list. Tap the record and choose "Reactivate". If, before closing, the client had sessions scheduled for after today (reschedules and extras), the app shows which ones and asks whether they stay on the calendar ("Keep") or go ("Discard"): the arrangement may have changed. If you discard one that was already paid, the app asks what to do with the amount (keep as credit, keep as paid or refund). If there are days off or vacation in the return period, the app asks whether to keep or cancel those sessions. If you had written off an amount for them, the app asks whether it goes back to To receive.

2.12 — Can I delete a record?

Yes, but be careful. Deleting removes the record and all associated financial history (payments received and paid months). Data is not preserved in the app after deletion — the only way to recover it is from a backup that contains it.

If you want to preserve the history, use "Close client" instead of delete.

2.13 — I have a client with zero value. Is that a problem?

No. Records with zero value are treated as free attendance. They don't generate charges and still appear in the schedule and session history — you track frequency normally. In the statistics, free sessions count toward the average ticket with a value of zero: free work is still work.

A free session that has already started still has Edit, Reschedule, Cancel and Delete, like any other: there is no payment to reverse.

If a new price starts applying to a free session that already happened, it goes back to awaiting payment at the new price: it is never marked paid without money. After the intake sessions, the ones that already happened keep the value they had, and the regular price applies to the ones to come.

2.14 — What is an event, and how do I manage it?

An event is a commitment that is not a session with a client: a conference, a class you teach, a supervision meeting. It shows in the schedule, in Today, in reminders and in days off, but stays out of the money (Outstanding, billing, statistics and documents). To create one, tap "+" and "Full record", at the end of the booking screen (or "New Client", in the Clients tab, while it is empty), and choose Event.

━━ The event page ━━

Tapping an event opens its page, with three tabs:

  • Dates: upcoming, past and cancelled. Tap a date to reschedule, cancel or restore it.
  • Schedule: the repeats (tap one to change its day, time, how it repeats or its end, or to stop repeating; "Repeat" on the last line) and the pauses — the same as a client's, including "on the last" (the last Thursday of the month, for example).
  • Profile: name, repetition, duration, virtual room, reminder, end and delete.

━━ What you can do with a day ━━

In Today, in the Schedule and on the page, the same actions: "Reschedule this day", "Cancel this day" (with the reason; the message has "Undo"), "Restore this day", "Join the room" (opens the link) and "Send reminder" (by WhatsApp). "Add a date" adds one more day outside the repetition, with no value. The swipes are the same as a client's: swipe right to send the reminder (upcoming) or cancel the day (past); swipe left to reschedule.

━━ All day ━━

An all-day event has no time on the screens and does not clash with the sessions of the day. The reminder goes out at 8:00 AM: on the day itself or on the days before that you choose.

━━ Past midnight ━━

Anything that goes past midnight shows on both days, as in Google and on the iPhone: on the first until midnight and, on the next, under "Continued from yesterday" (tap to open the day it started), in the day timeline and in the week grid. In the numbers, it belongs to the day it starts.

━━ Ending, and the event that has passed ━━

Ending removes the dates from now on from the schedule; past ones stay, and you can reactivate it later. A one-time event whose date has passed moves to "Past events", at the end of the Clients list, and does not count toward your plan's limit; giving it a new date brings it back.

2.15 — How do I record initial sessions?

When creating the record, under "Sessions", choose "Intake" (for teachers, "Trial"). You schedule the first one (date and time) and the initial session price — zero means free. The record starts "in initial sessions": the "Intake" (or "Trial") label shows on the cards, and you schedule the next ones one at a time ("Schedule a session", at the top of the profile, on the card and in the menu). During the phase, billing is per session, at the initial session price.

On the profile (and in the Today and Clients card menus), the two outcomes:

  • "Start regular sessions" opens the repeat already filled with the weekday and time of the last initial session, plus the billing and the price. On save, the phase closes, the repeat takes effect and the record becomes a regular client. With monthly billing and an initial session in the same month, the monthly fee starts next month (per session until then) — the form says so. With a new price per session and sessions from today on before the first one that repeats, the app asks from which one to charge: the earlier ones keep the price of the initial sessions, like the ones before, and the chosen one and the ones after it get the new price.
  • "Didn't continue" is the usual ending: the record goes to "Closed", and the profile keeps that the person didn't continue after the initial sessions.

Someone in initial sessions counts toward the plan limit. In the Insights, the Clients card says how many people started with initial sessions in the period and each outcome, and "Needs attention" reminds you of anyone in initial sessions for 30 days with no session scheduled. The activity log keeps both outcomes.

For an existing record without a repeat, "Repeat from this session" (in the menu of a session that took place) does the same as "Start": the repeat starts on the session's weekday and time.

2.16 — How do I change the day or time of a client's sessions?

On the record page, Schedule tab, tap the repeat: its page opens, with the day, the time, the repeat and when it ends. Change what you need and choose, before saving, when the change applies:

  • "From now on" (selected at first): applies from tomorrow on, or from the date you choose. The past stays as it is.
  • "Fix from the start": also fixes the past, for a repeat entered with the wrong day or time. Sessions with something recorded (a payment, a no-show, a note) stay on their day and count for their week: the new day doesn't enter that week, and nothing is doubled. Rescheduled and extra sessions stay as they are.

"Fix from the start" doesn't apply when the change would add sessions in the past or change the repeat of past ones (weekly to every other week, for example): the page says why, and "From now on" stays. If the outstanding amount changes, the app tells you how much before saving. At the bottom of the page, "Stop repeating on…", to choose the last day. To change several repeats at once, or switch to one-off sessions, use the Profile tab → "Recurring sessions".

━━ A new repeat ━━

"Repeat", on the last line of the repeats, opens the same page, to create one: when it starts, the time, the repeat (tap to see the options) and when it ends. Under "In your schedule", before saving, the app says whether the time is free in the coming weeks or who it overlaps with, shows other free times (one tap switches), the next dates and "See the week", to pick in your week; on a large screen, the week sits beside it. If the start has already passed, the page says how many sessions already happened and how much becomes outstanding, with "Start on …", the first date from now on.

━━ Customize the repeat ━━

"Customize...", at the end of the repeat options, opens the repeat page. Under "How it repeats", at the top, the repeat in plain words and the next dates, as you choose. Below, the number (with "−" and "+") and the unit: days, weeks, months or years ("every 2 years on October 13"). For days, "Monday to Friday only"; for months, which day (the day, the week of the month, the last one, the last day). In a new repeat ("Repeat" on the Schedule tab and a new record), you can mark more than one day of the week, like Monday and Thursday: each day you mark becomes a repeat, at the same time; the end applies to all of them, and "After 10 sessions" counts the sessions of all the days together. Tap "Use this repeat". The end isn't there: it's the "Ends" line, on the repeat page and on the repeat card of the form.

━━ Stop repeating ━━

On the profile of someone with a repeat, "Stop repeating": the next session stays booked, the app stops booking the ones after it, and the person stays in the app, with one-off sessions. What already happened stays as it is; if the outstanding amount changes (billing that falls due before the session), the app tells you how much first. With sessions already paid after the end, the app asks, as in a pause ("Already paid in this period"): keep as credit, keep as paid or refund. The button shows with billing per session; with monthly billing, in a group or in a block, use "Stop repeating on…" (or "Finish the block"). To stop on a day you choose, "Stop repeating on…", at the bottom of the repeat page. To repeat again, "Repeat", next to "Schedule a session" on the profile (2.17).

2.17 — I see people one-off: how do I book a session without the full record?

The "+" on Today and Clients opens "Schedule a session", a single screen:

  • Who: search by name, phone ("98765" finds the number) or email. Once you pick the person, the app suggests "like last time": the same weekday and time, at the next free slot. For someone new, "Someone new" asks for the name and phone right there; if the number already belongs to someone, the app warns you ("Use…" or "It's someone else") and never merges two records on its own.
  • When: the day, the time, the length and "Repeat", with "Free: no one booked at that time" (or who it overlaps with) and "See the week". Under "Repeat": "Doesn't repeat", "Every week", "Every two weeks" or "Every month", on the chosen day and time ("On Saturdays, 11:00 AM"; the month by the week of the day, like "On the second Saturday of every month"). When it repeats, the app books the next sessions on its own, starting from the first one, billed per session; you can stop it on the profile ("Stop repeating", 2.16). People who already have recurring sessions don't see "Repeat" (it's an extra session, 3.7), and neither does a session in the past.
  • Fee: for someone who came before, that day's price; for someone new, empty — with no fee, the app asks whether it's free. "Already paid" marks the session as paid when you book.
  • Reminders: your phone reminder and "Let them know on WhatsApp": after booking, the app shows the confirmation and asks if you want to send it; WhatsApp only opens with "Send on WhatsApp" (the text is in Settings → WhatsApp messages → "Confirmation when booking"). Nothing goes out on its own.

This works with any schedule: which one to pick in Settings → "Your schedule" and what it changes, in 1.10.

At the end of the screen and of the "Repeat" sheet, "Another repeat, monthly billing or a group: Full record" opens the usual record. The "Schedule a session" on the profile and on the cards of someone without a repeat opens the same screen, with the person already picked (and "Repeat" at "Doesn't repeat"). When it repeats, on Premium, someone who only had one-off sessions starts counting toward the limit (2.7). On the profile of someone without a repeat, "Repeat", next to "Schedule a session", opens the "Repeat" sheet with the day and time of the last session: the repeat starts after it, and what is already booked stays as it is.

In Clients, with one-off sessions or "Both", the order applies to the whole list, recurring and one-off together. In "Next session" (the default), everyone by the next one, with the days as headings ("TODAY", "TUESDAY", "10/31"), and, at the end, "Nothing booked", by the last visit ("last visit 6 weeks ago · usually every 4"); in "Last session", everyone by the last visit; in "A → Z", by name; in "Highest balance" and "Lowest balance", those with something outstanding first, then the others as in "Next session". Above the list, the filters, each with how many and only when someone is in it: "All", "Recurring" and "One-off" (with both kinds) and "Due for a session" (by the last visit); the filter stays until you leave the screen, and the search looks through everyone. As you scroll, "To receive" goes up with the list, and the filters stay pinned at the top. Each line says how the person comes, without the bolt: someone who repeats, the repeat ("today, 9:00 AM · every Saturday"); someone one-off, the session booked, with its services and price ("Consultation · …"; someone's first, with "First time"), or the last visit. How often someone comes shows with 3 sessions or more; when the last one was one and a half times that interval ago, or more, "Due for a session", for up to a year. Those who haven't come in over a year, with nothing booked or outstanding, are folded at the end of the list, under "No visit in over a year" (tap to open); search finds everyone. On the card, the chat icon reaches out on WhatsApp (the text is in Settings → WhatsApp messages → "Reach out on WhatsApp"; with something outstanding, it opens the charge) and the calendar icon opens "Schedule a session". The search also finds by phone.

On the profile of someone you see one-off, under the numbers, how often they come and the last visit ("Usually comes every 4 weeks · last visit on Tue, Sep 1, 6 weeks ago"), with "Due for a session" at the top; with nothing to charge or booked, "Reach out on WhatsApp" sits next to "Schedule a session". When you mark a one-off session as paid right when it happens, with nothing else booked, the app asks "Schedule the next one?", with a suggestion based on how often the person comes, checked against your schedule ("In 2 weeks, as usual · free"), "Every week at this time" (the repeat, on the session's day and time) or "Another day", which opens "Schedule a session"; "Not now" closes it. In the Panorama, "What needs attention" shows who is due.

2.18 — How do services work?

In Settings → "Services", add what you offer: the name, the price, the length and, if you like, a break after (to take notes, tidy up the room: when booking, the next time opens only after it). To change the order, touch and hold a service and drag it. With no services, everything stays as always: the length from Settings and each record's price.

With services, the "Schedule a session" screen (2.17) gets "What": pick one or more, and the length and the price add up (you can still change the price). Once you pick the person, "Like last time" brings the services from the last visit already checked, and "Schedule the next one?" repeats them. The session keeps the services as they were that day: changing a service's price or name later doesn't change the past.

On the Today and Schedule cards and in the record, the session shows its services ("$310.00 · Consultation + Body composition"), with no discount tag: the price came from them. In the service, you can also turn on an included follow-up and how soon people usually come back (2.19).

When you create your first service, the app asks whether you work in healthcare (the answer is yours and changes in Services, "I work in healthcare"). With "Yes", a tip under the name suggests a general name that doesn't say why the person comes ("Psychotherapy session", not "Depression treatment").

2.19 — How does the follow-up work?

In the service (2.18), turn on "Includes a follow-up" and choose the window (many use 30 days) and the price: no charge or with a price. A session with that service that happened, with no no-show, gives a follow-up until the end of the window — which moves with the days off and vacation inside it ("counting the vacation").

When you mark the session as paid, "Schedule the follow-up?" comes instead of "Schedule the next one?", with a day within the window. On the "Schedule a session" screen, whoever has a follow-up due sees, at the top of "What", "Follow-up to the session on 10/05": tap it to choose it; the follow-up takes half the service's length. In Clients, the card says "Follow-up by 11/04" (amber in the last 7 days), and the Panorama warns when the window is ending. In the record, the original session says "follow-up by 11/04" or, once booked, "follow-up on 10/20".

A follow-up with no charge is like a free session: it doesn't go into To receive and doesn't ask to be marked as paid. The app never charges for the follow-up on its own: once the window passes, it goes away, and booking a follow-up anyway means picking the service.

With your online booking page live (10.11), the follow-up can also go by link: in "Schedule the follow-up?", the third choice is "Send the follow-up link"; in the record, the button next to "Reach out on WhatsApp"; in Clients, the WhatsApp button of whoever has a follow-up due. The sheet has the message ready, with the deadline, plus "Send on WhatsApp", "Copy the link" and, when the session came through the page, "Send by email" (to that request's confirmed email). The link's page shows only the follow-up times, up to the deadline, without asking for a name or email. A follow-up booked there goes into your schedule on its own, at the follow-up price (no charge: not in To receive), and the same link shows the time, with "Reschedule" and "Cancel" up to your cancellation deadline; cancelled before the follow-up window ends, the link works again. If you book the follow-up in the app, or the session is cancelled or removed, the link stops working; a window that moves with a new day off goes to the link too. The server keeps only the length, the deadline and whether it's free of charge — never the name.

In "Come back in", say how soon people usually come back ("3 months"): until someone has a frequency (fewer than 3 visits), "Schedule the next one?" suggests the day by it, and "Due for a session" counts on it. A long frequency is said in months ("usually comes every 3 months").

In healthcare (2.18), with the currency set to the Brazilian real, a follow-up with a price shows, under the price, what CFM Resolution 1,958/2010 says: in medicine, a follow-up to review the tests ordered at the visit isn't charged. The price is yours.

2.20 — How does a session block work?

A block is an agreed number of sessions — an assessment, a package, therapy with a set number of sessions — with the progress "4 of 10" everywhere. The price is "Per session" — each session with its own price: the block only counts — or "The block price", one total for the whole block.

When adding someone new, under "Sessions", choose "Block": the name ("Neuropsychological assessment"), how many sessions and how they are booked — on the usual days and times (the repeat below, which ends by itself at the last one) or one at a time (the first now, the others when you agree) — and, below, "The price". For someone already in the app, on the profile › Schedule, "New block".

With the block price, choose the total (the app never fills in a price by itself) and how it is paid: all at the start, half at the start and half at the end, in instalments (2 to 12) or all at the end; the preview shows each instalment and when it is due. The 1st is due when the 1st session starts; the others, every month, on the same day; the one at the end, at the last agreed session ("More sessions" do not change it). An instalment already due stays due, even if its session changes later. A due instalment goes into To receive, Outstanding ("1 outstanding") and the usual payment sheet, with the credit and "Undo"; the receipt is per instalment ("Receipt for this instalment", when you tap the paid instalment), and in the Receita file each instalment is a payment; in the profile's History tab, it shows in the month the money came in. The sessions are "included in the block": no price of their own, never outstanding; "Mark as paid" in their menu receives the outstanding instalment. In the Statistics, each session is worth the price ÷ the agreed sessions.

On the profile, the label "Block 4 of 10" and "Schedule the 6th of 10". On the Schedule tab, the block line opens its page: the numbered sessions, what is left to book, "More sessions" (included in the block, no charge, or charged, like extras) and "Finish the block"; in a block with a price, "The price" at the top (the instalments: paid, outstanding, not due yet) and "End midway". On Today and in the Schedule, "16:00 · 5/10"; in Clients, the label.

With your online booking page live (10.11), in a block booked one at a time, the block page has "The block link": the sheet has the message ready, "Send on WhatsApp" and "Copy the link". The link’s page shows only the length and how many are left — never the person’s name or the block’s — and whoever opens it picks several times at once, up to the ones left, and the rest later, with the same link. Each session picked goes into your schedule on its own, as "Schedule the 6th of 10" would book it, and counts in the block; in a block with a price, it is included in it: no instalment is created or changed. Opened again, the link lists the sessions booked through it, each with its own link, to reschedule or cancel up to your cancellation deadline. If you book in the app, add "More sessions" or a session leaves the count, the link follows; once the block is finished, it stops working, and it goes away after 60 days without use. Sent it to the wrong person? "Change the link", below "Copy the link", stops the old one and creates another; the sessions already booked stay and show up in the new link, and the link of each one, from the old link, stops working too. If the block had already ended when someone booked through the link, the session comes in outside the block with no amount, and "Changes through the page" tells you: set the amount if needed. The server keeps only the length and how many are left — never the name.

In a block with a price, before the 1st session the page only offers "End midway"; finishing before the end does not change the price: in the Statistics, the session that closes the block takes the value of the ones that did not happen. "End midway" asks what is left to receive, with no answer preselected — charge only what happened, the whole block (with the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code warning) or write off the rest —; what was already paid stays paid, and an overpayment becomes credit or is refunded, as you choose. If the block’s count later changes — a payment undone on another device (before the cloud brings the two together), or a no-show that counts in the block again —, the block page shows the difference — how much left it as credit or refund and how much extra it received — and, under "Settle the difference", offers "Take it from the credit" or "Charge the difference" (which goes into the final instalment), with neither selected; with a refund, or with the credit already used, only "Charge the difference". Ending the record with a priced block under way asks this first. Billing also doesn't switch to monthly with a priced block under way: the app asks you to finish or end the block first, and monthly billing starts the month after its last session (the instalments still to come stay: they are for sessions that already happened). With the switch to monthly already set and a new priced block that falls in the middle of it, the app asks before saving the block: postpone monthly until after the block, or create the block without a price (the sessions stay in the monthly fee).

A no-show counts as a session of the block; in its menu, "Don't count in the block" takes it out of the count (the no-show stays recorded). A cancelled session does not count.

So does a day off: a session of the block that it cancels does not count, and a block booked by the repeat moves on — it gets the repeat's next date ("4 of 10 · 1 cancelled by the day off"). Remove the day off and the session comes back, and the extra date goes.

So does a pause with a return date: the sessions it takes do not count, and a block booked by the repeat gets the next dates; remove the pause and the extra dates go. Without a return date, the sessions stay to be booked until the return: when you reactivate, the block moves on.

Once the last agreed session is marked as paid (in a block with a price, when it ends, the first time Today opens after it), the app asks what comes next: "Finish the block" (the record moves to "Seen", with its history), "Start regular sessions" (a regular repeat) or "New block". On the Panorama, "What needs attention" tells you when the block is near the end ("The block ends in 2 sessions") and when it has stalled, with nothing scheduled.

2.21 — How do I find records with the same phone?

In Settings → Your data → "Records with the same phone" (and at the end of Clients, while there is something to check), one group per phone number — with or without the country code —, each record with its sessions and the last one; tap to open the record. It may be the same person added twice, or different people sharing one phone, like a mother of two kids.

The app never merges two records. If it is the same person, open the records and decide what to do with each (closing the extra one, for example: its history stays). If they are different people, "They’re different people" takes the group off the list, on this device; it comes back if someone else gets the same phone, and "Show again", at the end of the list, brings it back.

When you type the phone of someone new in "Schedule a session", the app already warns you when it belongs to someone (2.17).
