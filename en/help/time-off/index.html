Days off and vacation

Days off and vacation in Session Flow: adding them, Brazil's public holidays, letting people know, the free plan's limit and monthly billing while you're away.

7.1 — Day off vs vacation — what's the difference?

Just the label: when creating, pick Day off or Vacation under "Type" (Day off to start with); in the app they work the same. The name (optional) shows on the Schedule, on Today and on the sessions it cancels: in the profile, "Cancelled · Christmas"; without a name, the type ("Cancelled · day off").

7.2 — How do I add a day off?

On the Schedule screen, tap the beach umbrella at the top: it opens the list of days off, with the upcoming ones on top and the past ones collapsed at the end. Tap "+", select the days and confirm. Sessions scheduled within the period are automatically cancelled. With days that have already passed, the sheet tells you before you confirm how much leaves what's outstanding (the unpaid sessions on those days); when you change the dates of a day off, also what comes back.

7.3 — Does the free plan limit days off?

Yes. Up to 14 days per year, added one by one. On Premium it's unlimited and you can pick a full date range at once.

7.4 — I cancelled a day off. Do the sessions come back?

Yes. Sessions cancelled by the day off are automatically restored when the day off is removed. Whoever had been notified of the day off shows up in the list "Let them know the time is back" (question 7.7).

7.5 — I have a fixed-monthly client during my vacation. How do I bill?

The monthly amount is not adjusted automatically — you decide. To give a discount for that month only, use the "Month discount": in the profile, Sessions tab, tap the "⋯" on the month card (on fixed monthly, while the month hasn't been paid). "Update price" changes the price from that date on, so it doesn't fit a single month.

7.6 — What about already-paid sessions during the day off?

Already-paid sessions in the period are not cancelled by the day off (you've already received payment). The app warns you how many paid sessions exist before confirming.

A free session, which the app marks as paid on its own when it starts, doesn't count as paid: nobody paid anything for it. The day off cancels it like the others, also on days that have passed, and it comes back if the day off is removed. The same goes for a pause.

7.7 — How do I let people know about a day off?

When you confirm a day off that cancelled someone's session, the list "Notify who had a session" opens: one row per person, with the time (or the dates, when there are several) and "Notify". Tapping it opens WhatsApp with the "Day-off notice" from Settings → WhatsApp messages, ready to send: the day of the cancelled session and the return (the person's first session after the day off), without the day off's name. Without a number on the record (in a group, the participant's), WhatsApp opens for you to pick the contact. At the bottom, the button goes to the next one left; "Not now" closes.

The list also stays on the day off's row (Schedule → beach umbrella), which says how many people were notified, and on Today, on the day off.

"Notified" means you opened WhatsApp with the message: the app doesn't know whether it was sent. It stays on this device for 7 days and in the Activity log, which your other devices see through the cloud.

If the day off is removed, whoever had been notified shows up in the list "Let them know the time is back".

Whoever booked through your online booking page gets the "Booking cancelled" email, and their line says "Notified by email". If the day off is removed after the email, that booking stays cancelled (the person was already told), and the app says so. A day off doesn't ask who cancelled: you are the one cancelling, and, with a deposit, the email offers whoever paid a full refund or a credit.

7.8 — How do I mark holidays?

In Schedule → beach umbrella ("Vacation & Days Off") → "Holidays", or in Settings → Your work → "Holidays". The page shows the national holidays of the next 12 months and, separately, the optional days and city holidays: Carnival, Good Friday and Corpus Christi. Nothing comes checked: check the ones you don't work and tap the button at the bottom ("Mark 4 days off"). Each holiday becomes a day off with its name, and that day's sessions are cancelled, as in a day off marked by hand (Carnival, one day off on each of its two days). The notices to whoever had a session stay on each day off's row (7.7).

To work on a holiday again, uncheck it on the same page: the day off goes, and the sessions come back. A holiday already inside another day off (your vacation, for example) shows checked and stays as it is. State and city holidays: mark them as days off in "Vacation & Days Off".

On the free and Essential plans, holidays count toward the year's days off (7.3). The list is Brazil's: it shows with the Country set to Brazil (Settings → Date, time and currency → Country; on automatic, with the currency in reais or the phone in Brazil).
