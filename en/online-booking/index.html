Online booking

Premium Agenda

Clients book themselves, any time.

With Premium Agenda, you get a booking page on the internet, at agenda.getsessionflow.app/your-name. Clients see only your free times, pick theirs, and the time goes straight into your Session Flow schedule.

How it works

  • You choose your services and the hours you work.
  • You send the link on WhatsApp, on Instagram or on the QR code card.
  • Whoever books picks a free time. It goes into your schedule, and you both get the email.

To put the page online, the cloud needs to be on (that is where the page lives), with your address, your hours in "When you work", the name shown on the page and your cancellation window. On the Subscription screen, "See what the page looks like" shows an example before you subscribe.

Your page

The address is yours, with your photo or logo, a cover, a short introduction, your services (with their length and, if you like, the price) and the places where you work, in person or online. The link shows a preview of the page on WhatsApp.

Only your free times

The page only shows what is free: your appointments, days off and every event scheduled in the app make that time unavailable. You choose how far ahead people must book, how many days ahead they can see and, if you like, a daily limit. Whatever you book, cancel or change in the app reaches the page within seconds.

In "Calendars that block your page", events from the phone calendars you choose stop showing as free too. The app checks the calendar when it opens and once a day, and neither the name nor the time of the event leaves your phone.

Requests, in the app

You choose how requests come in: "Confirm right away" or "I approve each request", which holds the time until you answer. The Bookings tab shows what is waiting for your answer, what changed through the link and who booked on your page; you get an email notice for each request (or a daily summary).

None of the times work? "Suggest another time" sends the person up to 3 free times, held for them for up to 24 hours: they pick one through the link, without filling anything in again.

Emails for whoever books

  • a 6-digit code that confirms their email before booking;
  • the confirmation, with the booking link and a file for the phone calendar;
  • a reminder, with "I’ll be there" and "I need to reschedule";
  • a notice if the time changes or is cancelled.

Through the same link, they see the time, reschedule or cancel, within the window you set.

Cancellation window and deposit

Before booking, the page shows how late people can cancel or reschedule through their link. In Brazil, outside healthcare, it can also ask for a deposit through Pix, with your Pix key: the request waits, and you accept it when the payment arrives. The deposit goes straight to you, and Session Flow charges no fee per booking or payment.

In healthcare

  • the page doesn't ask for a deposit;
  • service prices start hidden (you can show them, service by service);
  • the page goes live with your professional license number, which it shows;
  • "Show on Google" only turns on with your license number;
  • the app suggests a general name for each service, one that doesn't reveal why someone is coming.

"I work in healthcare" is your answer, in Settings › Services.

Fully booked: the waiting list

On a day you work, with no free time left for the chosen service, the page offers "Let me know if a time opens up". If a time opens that day, everyone waiting gets an email, and the first to book gets it. Each person gets at most 3 notices, and the list clears at the end of the day.

Follow-ups and blocks by link

A follow-up included in a service goes out as a link that only shows times within its window, with no name or email to fill in. A block booked one session at a time (an assessment, a package) has its own link: the person picks several times at once, up to the sessions left.

Reaching new clients

The QR code card comes ready to print. "Show on Google" starts off: only people with the link reach your page; turned on, it can show up in searches, and each person's own links never do.

Privacy

With online booking on, your clients' requests — the name, the contact details and the time — go through our server, and you get the notices by email. Without it, none of this happens. Free times are worked out on your device, and the page uses no advertising, pixels or usage analytics; against bots, it uses Google's reCAPTCHA.

You are the controller of the data of the people who book on your page, and Session Flow is the processor. The details are in the Privacy Policy (section 4.9) and the Cloud Terms of Use (section 6).

The plan

Online booking is part of Premium Agenda: everything in Premium plus your booking page. Monthly or yearly, with 14 days free on every paid plan, monthly or yearly, through the store. You subscribe in the app on your phone or tablet, and prices show up there, on the Subscription screen. If you are on Premium, you switch plans, and the store uses what was left of it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does online booking cost?

It's part of Premium Agenda. Prices show up in the app, on the Subscription screen, in your local currency, and the 14 free days apply monthly or yearly, through the store.

Do my clients need to install anything?

No. The page opens in the browser on a phone or computer. To book, they confirm their email with a 6-digit code.

What if someone books a time I just took in the app?

The session comes in anyway (they already got the confirmation), and the app lets you know: the notice opens the session so you can reschedule or cancel it, and the person who booked hears about it by email.

Can I approve each request first?

Yes. With "I approve each request", the time is held until you answer, and the request says until when. With "Confirm right away", it goes straight into your schedule.

Does the page show up on Google?

Only if you turn on "Show on Google", in Settings › Online booking › How you appear. In healthcare, it only turns on with your professional license number.
