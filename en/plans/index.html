Plans

A plan for every stage of your business.

Start free, with up to 4 clients. When you need more, Essential, Premium and Premium Agenda come monthly or yearly, with 14 days free on every paid plan, monthly or yearly, through the store.

The four plans

 

Free

To get started

  • Up to 4 active clients
  • Full calendar and reminders
  • "Schedule a session" and one-off sessions
  • Import from your phone calendar
  • PDF receipts and letters
  • WhatsApp reminders
  • Activity log and full backup

 

Essential

Monthly or yearly

14 days free

  • Everything in Free
  • Up to 40 active clients
  • Full statistics
  • Settlement history
  • Reminders and documents without Session Flow branding

Recommended

Premium

Monthly or yearly

14 days free

  • Everything in Essential
  • Up to 1000 active clients, and unlimited occasional clients
  • Cloud sync across phone, tablet and computer
  • Insights: your business in charts, on a big screen
  • WhatsApp payment reminders listing unpaid sessions
  • Groups, prepayments and discounts
  • Unlimited vacations and time off, by date range

Most complete

Premium Agenda

Monthly or yearly

14 days free

  • Everything in Premium
  • Your online booking page
  • Clients pick their time, 24/7
  • Only your free times show up
  • Booked times go into your schedule
  • Email confirmation and reminder
  • Waiting list and a printable QR code card

14 days free on every paid plan, monthly or yearly, through the store. Prices show up in the app, on the Subscription screen, in your local currency. You subscribe in the app on your phone or tablet, through the App Store or Google Play.

Monthly or yearly

All three paid plans have both options. On the Subscription screen in the app, each plan shows the monthly and the yearly price in your local currency. If you already subscribe, you see your current plan and can switch period ("Switch to yearly", "Switch to monthly").

The free trial

14 days free on every paid plan, monthly or yearly, through the store. The App Store or Google Play runs the trial: it starts when you subscribe in the app. To avoid paying, cancel in the store before the trial ends (on iPhone, at least 24 hours before). If you don't, the store starts charging at the end of the 14 days.

Where to see prices

Prices vary by country, and the store shows and charges them. You see them in the app, in Settings › Subscription, in your local currency, before you subscribe.

Where to subscribe

You subscribe in the app on your phone or tablet, through the App Store or Google Play. On Premium and Premium Agenda, with the cloud on, your plan works in your browser too, at app.getsessionflow.app.

Switching plans

Moving from Premium to Premium Agenda is a plan change: the store ends Premium and uses what was left of it, without charging for both. On the way back, from Premium Agenda to Premium or Essential, the app first shows what you would lose.

Cancelling

Through the store: on Android, Google Play › Subscriptions; on iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Your data stays: the app asks you to choose 4 clients for the Free plan, and cloud data is kept for 90 days after the plan ends.

Frequently asked questions

How much do the plans cost?

Prices show up in the app, on the Subscription screen, in your local currency: they vary by region, and the store charges them. The Free plan costs nothing, for up to 4 active clients.

Which plans have the free trial?

Every paid plan: Essential, Premium and Premium Agenda, monthly or yearly, for 14 days, through the store.

What's the difference between Premium and Premium Agenda?

Premium Agenda has everything in Premium plus your online booking page, at agenda.getsessionflow.app/your-name, with email confirmations and reminders, a waiting list and a printable QR code card.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. From Premium to Premium Agenda, the store uses what was left of the plan before. From monthly to yearly, "Switch to yearly", on the Subscription screen.

I cancelled. Is my data gone?

No. What is on your device stays; the app asks you to choose 4 clients for the Free plan, and cloud data is kept for 90 days after the plan ends.
