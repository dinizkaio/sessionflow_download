How it works

Three steps,
then the month runs itself.

  1. Add your client

    Name, fee and the rule: weekly, every other week, monthly or group. Just once.

  2. The calendar fills in

    The month's sessions appear on their own. Holidays and time off cancel the ones that fall on them.

  3. Get paid, stay on top

    One tap marks a session as paid. Anything still open goes into your total to collect.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Without the cloud, we don't receive, see or store what you record. The cloud is optional, part of Premium, and only works if you turn it on.

Do I need an account?

No. An account (Google or Apple) only comes in if you turn on the cloud, to sync your devices.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes, on Premium with the cloud on: open app.getsessionflow.app in your browser and sign in with the same account. It's the same data as on your phone.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes, and on iPad and Android tablets too.

How does the free trial work?

When you choose Premium in the app, you start with 14 days free. After that, the subscription continues through the store unless you cancel before.

How do I cancel?

Through the store. On Android: Google Play › Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. The store manages the subscription.

Can it make superbills?

Yes. Documents include session receipts, attendance letters and a superbill template your clients can use to request insurance reimbursement.

Is Session Flow a clinical record?

No. There are no fields for diagnoses or clinical notes: it keeps what your calendar and payments need.

Can I charge monthly or for groups?

Yes. Charge per session or per month, and the app does the math. Groups, with each member's fee, are part of Premium.
