How it works

Three steps,
and the app does the rest.

  1. Schedule the session

    The "+" opens "Schedule a session": who it is (or someone new, with just a name and phone number), the day and time and, if it repeats, weekly, every other week or monthly. Or bring it all from your phone calendar.

  2. Your calendar fills in

    The month's sessions show up on their own. Mark a holiday or your time off, and the app cancels the sessions on those days.

  3. Get paid, stay on top of it

    One tap marks a session as paid. Anything unpaid goes into your total to collect.

Online booking

Premium Agenda

Clients book themselves,
any time.

With Premium Agenda, you get a booking page on the internet. Clients see only your free times, pick theirs, and the time goes straight into your Session Flow schedule.

agenda.getsessionflow.app/your-name

  • Only your free times

    Your appointments, days off and the events in the app block the time; if you like, your phone calendar does too.

  • Right away or with your OK

    Requests come straight in or wait for your answer in the Bookings tab, with an email notice for each one.

  • Emails for whoever books

    A code to confirm their email, the confirmation, a reminder with "I’ll be there" and "I need to reschedule", and a notice if the time changes.

  • Fully booked? Waiting list

    The page offers "Let me know if a time opens up". When a time opens that day, everyone waiting gets an email.

  • Your cancellation window

    You choose how late people can cancel through their link.

  • Your way

    Photo, cover, services and prices, a printable QR code card, and "Show on Google", if you turn it on.

In healthcare, the page doesn't ask for a deposit, prices start hidden, and it goes live with your professional license number.

Insights

Premium

The big picture
of your business.

On a computer, iPad or tablet, Insights shows your last year of work and what's coming up. Each chart states its takeaway in plain words, with a line on how to read it.

  • Your year, month by month

    What came in each month, your best month and the average, compared with the year before when there's enough history.

  • Your reminders

    Who is back from a break, who missed the last two sessions, a block close to its end, prepaid credit running out and upcoming birthdays. The "×" clears what you saw.

  • Your schedule by day and hour

    The time slots that fill up every week and the ones that stay open, over the last 13 weeks, plus the hours you worked.

  • Who joined and who left

    How many clients started, how many ended and how many stayed past the first three months.

  • How fast you get paid

    Out of every 100 payments, how many arrive by the day of the session, and how much of what's unpaid is old.

  • For one-off work

    What a session brings in, who came for the first time, the next visit and the next 14 days. With both kinds of schedule, a filter picks the view.

On Premium and Premium Agenda, on big screens: on a computer in your browser, with the cloud, and on iPad and tablets.

Cloud sync

Premium

Phone for the day,
computer for the month.

With the cloud on, your phone, tablet and computer always match. Marked a payment on your phone? It already shows up in your browser.

  • The app on your computer

    Open app.getsessionflow.app in your browser and sign in with the same Google or Apple account. Nothing to install.

  • Built for big screens

    Your client list and profile side by side, week and month views of your calendar, documents with a preview alongside, and Insights.

  • Every device in sync

    Syncing happens on its own. New phone? Sign in with the same account and your data comes right down.

  • Every change on record

    The Activity log shows what changed, when and on what kind of device, and you see it on all of them.

The cloud is part of Premium and Premium Agenda. It syncs and doesn't replace backups, so keep exporting copies from Backup & Restore.

How the cloud works

Phone calendar

On every plan

Bring your calendar over.
No retyping.

Session Flow reads your phone's calendar, with the accounts you already use there (Google, iCloud, Outlook), and turns recurring appointments into clients with their schedule set up. You review each person before anything comes in.

  • You choose

    No calendar is selected for you. You pick where to bring them from, and from what date.

  • One card per person

    Each person becomes a card: session, event or skip. Adjust the name, the fee and how you charge right there.

  • Existing clients stay as they are

    Same days and times? The client is simply linked to the calendar. Their history and payments don't change.

  • Check back later

    Something changed in your calendar? The app shows what changed there, a moved session, a deleted one, a new time, and you decide one by one.

The app only reads: nothing changes in your calendar, and nothing leaves your phone. On every plan, on Android and iPhone. On Premium Agenda, the calendars you choose also block times on your booking page.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone unless you want it to.

Without the cloud, everything stays on your device: we don't receive, see or store what you record in Session Flow.

  • No account requiredUse the app with no sign-up and no login.
  • No ads, no trackersNo advertising, usage analytics or tracking tools.
  • Optional cloudOn Premium and Premium Agenda, only if you turn it on: a copy on Google Cloud in São Paulo, Brazil, to sync your devices.
  • Online bookingOn Premium Agenda, with your page on, your clients' requests (the name, the contact details and the time) go through our server. Without it, none of this happens.
  • You're in controlTurn the cloud off, delete its data or your account from within the app, anytime.
  • We don't sell dataNot yours, and not that of the people who book on your page.

Session Flow is not a clinical record: there are no fields for diagnoses or clinical notes. Read the Privacy Policy

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Without the cloud, we don't receive, see or store what you record. The cloud is optional, part of Premium and Premium Agenda, and only works if you turn it on. With online booking, your clients' requests go through our server.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need an account (Google or Apple) to turn on the cloud, which syncs your devices and, on Premium Agenda, puts your booking page online.

What is online booking?

Your booking page on the internet, at agenda.getsessionflow.app/your-name: clients see only your free times, book on their own, and the time goes into your schedule, with an email confirmation and reminder. It's part of Premium Agenda.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes, on Premium and Premium Agenda with the cloud on: open app.getsessionflow.app in your browser and sign in with the same account. It's the same data as on your phone, on a screen made for computers, with week and month views and Insights.

What is Insights?

A screen with the charts of your business for computers, iPads and tablets: what came in each month, how fast you get paid, your schedule by day and hour, who joined and who left, and what needs your attention this week. It's part of Premium and Premium Agenda.

Can I bring in my Google or iPhone calendar?

Yes. In Settings › Phone calendar, the app reads your phone's calendar, with the accounts on it, and sets up your clients with you, one person at a time. It only reads: nothing changes in your calendar and nothing leaves your phone. On every plan.

Is the cloud a backup?

No, it syncs. Anything you delete on one device goes away on the others. To keep a copy, keep exporting backups from Backup & Restore.

Is it available for iPhone and Android?

Yes, and for iPad and Android tablets too.

How does the free trial work?

The trial is run by the store. When you subscribe to any paid plan in the app (Essential, Premium or Premium Agenda, monthly or yearly), the App Store or Google Play gives you 14 days at no charge. To avoid paying, cancel in the store before the trial ends (on iPhone, at least 24 hours before); if you don't, the store starts charging for the subscription.

How much does it cost?

Prices show up in the app, on the Subscription screen, in your local currency: they vary by region, and the store charges them. The Free plan costs nothing, for up to 4 clients.

How do I cancel?

Through the store. On Android: Google Play › Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. The store manages the subscription.

Can it create superbills?

Yes. Documents include session receipts, attendance letters and a superbill template your clients can use to request insurance reimbursement.

Is Session Flow a clinical record?

No. There are no fields for diagnoses or clinical notes: it only keeps what your calendar and payments need.

Can I charge monthly or for groups?

Yes. Charge per session or per month, and the app does the math. Groups, with each member's fee, are part of Premium.
