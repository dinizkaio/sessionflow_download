What's new in Session Flow

The app is updated often. These are the notes of each version, newest first.

Version 1.5.130 October 7, 2026

App icon on the launch screen. Reminders use newer Android and iPhone libraries. On the profile, ended rules and finished pauses are collapsed. The last-session warning reads the pause by its period. Full review, no regression. Activity log and spreadsheet: a past line dated by a day has no time. Over four sessions at the same time scroll sideways; in the week, “+N” opens the list. Intake sessions in Insights (Panorama). One-off sessions; extra session in Today; event pages.
